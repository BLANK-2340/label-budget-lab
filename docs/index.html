SAVED EXPERIMENT / SELECTED SEED

Accuracy versus annotation budget

The learning rule is deliberately small.

Three-parameter logistic regression, mean binary cross-entropy and L2 on weights only. Every fit starts at zero and runs 400 full-batch updates at rate 0.5, L2 0.02. All strategies share the initial labels, pool and test set. The curved task exposes a misspecified linear model.

More deliberate queries do not always win.

At 40 labels across three seeds, uncertainty has the highest mean accuracy on the linear task; uncertainty plus diversity leads on the curved task. Diversity alone trails random sampling in both. Three paired synthetic seeds do not establish significance or a universal acquisition advantage.

Read fixed protocol, measured results and limitations ↗

Original 2D synthetic data only, 8% oracle label flips. Budgets 8–40 include initial labels; batches of eight; 96 pool and 256 independent test points. Unqueried labels exist in the public synthetic audit bundle but are not passed to the fit. Recorded acquisition does not change in response to your interactions.
